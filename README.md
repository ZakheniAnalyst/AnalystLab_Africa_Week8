# AnalystLab_Africa_Week8

# HealthConnect Clinic — Data Analytics & Decision Support



##  Project Overview

The **HealthConnect Clinic Experience Lab** was an 8-week multidisciplinary project completed as part of the **AnalystLab Africa Experience Lab Internship Programme**.

The project explored how **data and AI can be used to reduce missed appointments, improve patient appointment attendance, make better use of available appointment slots, and provide more effective administrative support to patients.**

My contribution was within the **Data Analytics track**, where I worked on transforming appointment data into actionable insights through data analysis, KPI development, dashboarding, validation, and business recommendations.

The project progressed through:

**Problem Understanding → Analysis → Development → Integration → Testing → Refinement → Final Integration → Presentation**

---

##  Business Problem

HealthConnect Clinic wanted to better understand appointment attendance and no-show behaviour.

The central project question was:

> **How can HealthConnect Clinic use data and AI to reduce missed appointments and improve the patient support experience?**

The analysis focused on identifying patterns that could help HealthConnect understand:

- Appointment attendance and no-show behaviour
- Patient and appointment characteristics
- Reminder-related patterns
- Waiting times
- Distance to the clinic
- Previous appointment history
- Cancellation patterns
- Operational factors associated with missed appointments

---

##  Analytical Workflow

### 1. Data Preparation

The dataset was inspected and prepared for analysis by reviewing:

- Data types
- Missing values
- Duplicate records
- Inconsistent values
- Relevant analytical variables

Data-quality observations were documented before proceeding with analysis.

---

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis

Exploratory analysis was performed to understand appointment attendance and no-show patterns.

Key areas investigated included:

- Appointment characteristics
- Patient characteristics
- Previous appointment history
- Reminder information
- Waiting time
- Distance to clinic
- Cancellations
- Attendance outcomes

---

### 3. KPI Development

Relevant KPIs were developed to provide a concise view of appointment performance.

The KPIs were subsequently validated during the testing and refinement stages to ensure that dashboard figures were consistent with the underlying data.

---

### 4. Dashboard Development

A Power BI dashboard was developed to communicate the most important analytical findings.

The dashboard was progressively refined throughout the project based on:

- Analytical findings
- KPI validation
- Dashboard testing
- Visualisation improvements
- Business interpretation

The final dashboard was designed to provide decision-makers with a clear view of appointment attendance and no-show patterns.

---

##  Key Analytical Areas

The final analysis focused on identifying:

### Appointment Attendance

Understanding overall attendance behaviour and identifying patterns associated with missed appointments.

### No-Show Behaviour

Examining characteristics and appointment factors associated with patients failing to attend scheduled appointments.

### Patient & Appointment Segments

Comparing attendance patterns across relevant patient and appointment segments.

### Operational Factors

Investigating factors such as:

- Waiting time
- Distance to clinic
- Reminder channels
- Appointment characteristics
- Previous appointment behaviour

### Business Impact

Translating analytical findings into recommendations that could support HealthConnect's operational decision-making.

---

##  Business Insights & Recommendations

The analytical findings were translated into actionable recommendations for HealthConnect.

The recommendations were designed around:

- Improving appointment attendance
- Supporting targeted reminder strategies
- Identifying higher-risk appointment segments
- Improving the use of appointment capacity
- Supporting data-driven administrative decisions
- Using validated analytical findings alongside the wider HealthConnect solution

> **Important:** Recommendations are based on the available project dataset and should not be interpreted as clinical or medical advice.

---

##  Testing & Validation

An important part of the later stages of the project was validating the analytical outputs rather than simply presenting the initial findings.

Testing included:

- KPI calculation validation
- Dashboard figure validation
- Dashboard filter testing
- Cross-checking dashboard values against the underlying data
- Reassessing attendance/no-show findings
- Testing findings across relevant segments
- Reviewing analytical limitations
- Refining visualisations
- Re-testing important outputs after refinement

This helped ensure that the final analytical output was accurate, consistent and useful for decision-making.
